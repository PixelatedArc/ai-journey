After Week 1: 
Checked my portfolio site's scores. 

**1. Headline – 6 / 10**  
Why: It tells *who* you are and where you’re based, but it says nothing about *what* the visitor gains. “Designer, developer and motion artist” is a job‑title list, not a promise. No benefit, no urgency, no differentiation.

**2. CTA – 5 / 10**  
Why: You have two primary buttons (“See the work” / “Start a project”) competing for attention and neither tells the visitor *why* they should click. “Start a project” is vague, “See the work” is passive. No clear next‑step, no incentive, no scarcity.

**3. Trust signals – what’s missing**  
- **Client logos** (recognizable brands, not just “Pakistan, United States…” list).  
- **Concrete testimonial snippets** with name, photo, title, and a quantifiable result.  
- **Case‑study highlights** (e.g., “Reduced load time 40 % for XYZ Dental”).  
- **Security / money‑back guarantee badge** (or “100 % satisfaction guarantee”).  
- **Media mentions** (e.g., featured in *Webflow Blog*, *Smashing Magazine*).  
- **Professional credentials** displayed prominently (Webflow Practitioner badge, etc.) rather than buried in a list.

**4. Three quick fixes (rewritten copy)**  

| Issue | Before | After (concise, benefit‑driven) |
|-------|--------|---------------------------------|
| **Headline** | “Designer, developer and motion artist · Pakistan, working worldwide” | **“Turn your brand into a living website – design, code & motion, all done by one expert.”** |
| **Primary CTA** | “See the work” / “Start a project” | **Single button:** **“See the results →”** (sub‑text: “Real traffic, conversion & speed gains”) |
| **Trust signal block** | Long list of tools, certifications, country flags | **“Trusted by 30+ brands worldwide – 40 % faster load times, 50 % traffic lift, 40 % more conversions.”** <br>*Add 3‑4 logo thumbnails underneath.* |

**Implementation tip:** Put the revised headline at the top, the single CTA directly below it, and the trust‑signal line right under the CTA. This creates a clear *problem → solution → proof → action* flow that forces a conversion decision.
